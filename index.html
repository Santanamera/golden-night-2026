✦ GOLDEN NIGHT ✦

The stars aligned, the night was planned,
A dance beneath the golden band.
But seasons turn, and time must flow,
The pages turn, the wheel must slow.
What shines so bright will shine again,
When summer's light returns to reign.
Hold on to dreams, they will not fade—
For golden nights are not delayed.

We look forward to celebrating with you soon.
